SHANER & CO.

A design practice coordinating architects, designers, engineers, and consultants to translate ambition and utility into built environments specific to their context.

Committed to delivering clean and restrained work, driven by meaningful discourse with clients. Engaging from concept through construction, leading a project or integrating with an existing team.

S&CO. was founded by Evan Shaner in 2023 following a decade of design experience at internationally renowned architecture firms.
